# Quick start

Use this short workflow to make an AI-assisted task easier to direct, review, and repeat.

## 1. Define the result

State the decision or artifact you need, who will use it, and what “done” looks like. Prefer observable outcomes such as “compare these three options in a table and recommend one against the stated criteria.”

## 2. Provide the right evidence

Name the files, records, or sources the work may use. Identify the source of truth, relevant dates, and known gaps. Ask for material claims to be tied to evidence and for assumptions to be labeled.

## 3. Set boundaries

Specify the scope, constraints, and actions that are allowed. Make approval points explicit for external writes, production changes, purchases, disclosure, or other consequential actions. Share only information permitted by your organization.

## 4. Choose the response shape

Request the sections, format, length, and level of detail that make the result usable. For repeatable work, include a checklist or schema.

## 5. Review and verify

Check the result against source material and acceptance criteria. For important workflows, test representative normal cases, edge cases, and failure cases. Ask the model to report what it did not verify.

## Copy-ready starter

```text
Goal: [decision or artifact, audience, and intended use]

Evidence: Use [approved files or sources]. Treat [source] as authoritative.
State important gaps and label assumptions.

Constraints: Stay within [scope]. Do not [prohibited actions].
Get approval before [external, consequential, or irreversible actions].

Acceptance criteria: [observable checks for a useful and correct result]

Return: [sections, format, and length]

Before finishing, verify the result against the evidence and criteria.
List material uncertainties and anything not checked.
```

## Keep product details current

Model availability, settings, tools, privacy controls, and API behavior can change. Confirm those details in the [official documentation](official-resources.md) for the product and account you actually use.
